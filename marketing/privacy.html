Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 February 2026  ·  Effective: 1 February 2026

FLOWRA Suite ("FLOWRA", "we", "us", or "our") is operated by FLOWRA Technologies, registered in India. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect when you use any of our products — Insights, Tasks, Loyalty, or any future tool — and how we keep it safe. We comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

1. Information we collect

1.1 Account information

  • Name, business name, email address, mobile number, and login password (stored as a one-way bcrypt hash — we never see your plaintext password)
  • Role (admin, employee, salesman, dispatch, CA, etc.) and the company you belong to
  • GSTIN, billing address, and PAN — only when you enable invoicing or paid plans

1.2 Business data synced from Tally / Busy

When you connect our desktop sync agent to your Tally or Busy installation, we receive and store:

  • Sales, purchase, receipt, payment, journal, and credit/debit-note vouchers
  • Customer and creditor ledger names, balances, contact details, and addresses
  • Inventory items, stock-on-hand, and group hierarchies
  • Income, expense, asset, liability, and capital ledger balances

We do not read or store unrelated files on your computer. The agent only queries Tally / Busy via their official ODBC / XML interfaces and only on the schedule you configure.

1.3 Usage and telemetry

  • Page views, clicks, and feature usage — to improve product experience
  • Browser type, OS, IP address, and device fingerprint — for security and fraud detection
  • Sync agent version, last-sync timestamp, error logs, and Tally / Busy company name — to keep your sync healthy

1.4 What we don't collect

  • We don't sell, rent, or share your data with third-party advertisers
  • We don't read your email inbox, SMS, or contacts
  • We don't track you across other websites

2. How we use your data

  • Provide the service — render dashboards, run analytics, generate reports, send WhatsApp / email reminders you've configured
  • Keep your account secure — detect suspicious logins, brute-force attempts, and unauthorized access
  • Improve our products — aggregate, anonymized usage analytics; never your raw books
  • Customer support — when you contact us, we look at your account to help you
  • Legal compliance — respond to lawful requests from Indian government authorities

3. Where your data lives

All your business data is stored on encrypted servers in Mumbai, India (ap-south-1 / bom1 region). We don't transfer your books outside India unless you explicitly enable a feature that requires it (e.g., third-party WhatsApp BSP based abroad — we'll always show a clear consent prompt first).

4. Encryption and security

  • All data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3
  • Database volumes are encrypted at rest with AES-256
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (cost factor 12)
  • Multi-tenant isolation — every database query is scoped by tenant_id and company_id; no cross-customer data access is possible
  • Daily encrypted backups are retained for 30 days; weekly snapshots for 12 months

Read more on our Security page.

5. Sharing with third parties

We share your data only with these categories of sub-processors, all under contractual data-protection obligations:

  • Cloud infrastructure — DigitalOcean (Mumbai), MongoDB Atlas (Mumbai)
  • Email delivery — Resend, SendGrid (only the messages you send)
  • WhatsApp messaging — only if you enable WhatsApp Automation, via your selected BSP
  • Payments — Stripe / Razorpay (only billing details, never your books)
  • Analytics — privacy-respecting analytics (Plausible / self-hosted Umami) — no third-party trackers

6. Your rights under the DPDP Act

You have the right to:

  • Access — get a copy of all data we hold about you and your business
  • Correct — fix inaccurate or outdated personal information
  • Erase — delete your account and all associated data within 30 days of request
  • Withdraw consent — disable any feature that requires data sharing (WhatsApp, email blasts, etc.)
  • Grievance redressal — write to us; we'll respond within 7 working days
  • Nominate — designate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf in case of incapacity

To exercise any right, email privacy@flowralive.in from your registered email address.

7. Data retention

  • While your account is active — we retain your data to serve you
  • If you cancel — your books remain accessible for 90 days for export, then permanently deleted
  • Backup tapes — purged within 30 days of account deletion
  • Statutory records (invoices, KYC) — retained for 8 years per Indian tax law

8. Cookies

We use only essential cookies for login session, CSRF protection, and theme preference. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can clear cookies in your browser settings without losing access to your account.

9. Children

FLOWRA is built for businesses. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe we have, write to us and we'll delete it immediately.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as we ship new features. Material changes will be announced via in-app notice and email at least 14 days before they take effect.
