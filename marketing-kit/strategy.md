## 8. Content rules (do / don't)

**Do**:
- Lead with a problem the user *literally said in their last meeting* ("salesman gaya kahan?")
- Use ₹ — not $ — and Indian SME numbers (₹2L, ₹40k, ₹5,00,000)
- Bilingual headlines win 1.4× CTR over English-only
- Show the actual product UI in at least 1 in 3 posts
- Cite Tally + Busy by name — that's our parity moat

**Don't**:
- Don't gate-keep with email-form first
- Don't post stock photos of "businessmen handshake"
- Don't post on auto-pilot at 9 AM — IG SME peak is 7–9 PM IST
- Don't run lead forms without WhatsApp follow-up under 30 min — leads cool fast

## 11. Monthly review cadence

Every month-end, look at:
1. Top 3 posts by **WhatsApp clicks** (not likes) — repeat the *hook* with new visual
2. Bottom 3 posts by saves — kill the format, not the topic
3. Reel completion rate ≥ 60% → boost as ad. < 30% → re-edit hook
4. Demo no-show rate — if > 30%, tighten qualification ("Tally/Busy?" question)
